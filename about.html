Who We Are

Lighting Up Bihar & Beyond

Nova Lite Solution is a trusted LED lighting brand headquartered in Kishanganj, Bihar. We specialize in manufacturing and distributing energy-efficient LED products designed for homes, offices, shops, and industrial spaces.

Our mission is simple: deliver brilliant, durable and affordable lighting solutions to every corner of India. With a growing network of dealers and distributors across Bihar and neighbouring states, Nova Lite Solution is rapidly becoming the go-to brand for quality LED products.
